The 50-30-20 Budgeting Rule

A simple framework: 50% Needs, 30% Wants, 20% Savings & Investments

Your Monthly Income (post-tax):
Category % Amount (₹) What It Covers
NEEDS 50% ₹25,000 Rent, EMIs, groceries, insurance, utilities, transport, medical

SAVINGS 20% ₹10,000 SIP, PPF, NPS, emergency fund, FD, stocks, gold

Pro Tips

  • Increase SIP by ₹500 every 6 months (step-up SIP doubles your corpus)
  • Build emergency fund = 6 months expenses BEFORE investing
  • Term insurance (₹1Cr cover) costs just ₹600-800/month at age 30
  • Use credit card rewards to save ₹10-15K/year
  • Tracking alone reduces spending by 10-15% — fill this monthly!
  • If savings rate < 20%, cut WANTS first, never NEEDS
